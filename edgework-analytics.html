01Overview

One pipeline. Every game day.

Edgework Analytics automatically refreshes schedules, box scores, Statcast data, player statistics, rosters, starting lineups, and sportsbook odds. It turns that changing information into reproducible predictions, price comparisons, and a deployable dashboard.

02Problem

Baseball data does not stand still.

Predictions depend on information that arrives at different times. Probable pitchers change, lineups are confirmed shortly before first pitch, and sportsbook prices move throughout the day.

The system needed to remain useful when sources were late or partial, resume safely after interruption, and preserve exactly what was known when each prediction was produced.

03System design

A resumable path from source to screen.

01IngestMLB, Statcast, rosters, odds
02NormalizePostgreSQL point-in-time data
03PredictMulti-window calibrated models
04EvaluatePrice and expected-value checks
05PublishArchive and private dashboard

Each stage is designed to be rerunnable and resumable. Historical tables support research and training, while point-in-time datasets preserve the information available at prediction time. Probable-pitcher and projected-lineup logic lets the pipeline work before official lineups arrive and update when better information becomes available.

04Modeling

Probabilities first. Decisions second.

Model

Multi-window signals

Features capture performance across multiple time horizons rather than relying on one fixed snapshot.

Calibration

Honest probabilities

Outputs are calibrated so a probability can be evaluated as a probability rather than only as a ranking.

Markets

Price comparison

Moneyline and run-line prices are compared across sportsbooks using expected value. Totals remain experimental.

05Data integrity

Past predictions stay in the past.

Every published prediction is written to an immutable snapshot archive. Later data corrections, model updates, and price movement cannot rewrite what the system actually knew or recommended at that moment.

↳

This creates a defensible record for honest backtesting, monitoring, and performance review.

06Dashboard

Analysis, made usable.

The pipeline generates and deploys a private dashboard that brings model probabilities, market prices, and archived results into one view.

07Lessons learned

The model is only one part of the product.

01Reliable ingestion and clear failure recovery matter as much as model sophistication.

02Point-in-time correctness is essential when data and prices change after publication.

03A useful prediction product must communicate uncertainty, timing, and price, not just a pick.
